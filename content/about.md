Hey, I'm Ayoub, a computer science student at Xidian University, currently
heading into my junior year. My coursework has covered a wide range of
CS fundamentals (networking, computer architecture, data structures and
algorithms), but cybersecurity is the direction I'm actually chasing.

**Patch Notes** is where I document that chase, honestly, and in real time.

I'm not waiting until I've "made it" to start writing. Most technical blogs
you find online are written by people who already have the answers. This one
is different: it's a running log of what I'm learning, what's breaking, what's
clicking, and what isn't.

Why public? A few reasons:

- **Accountability.** Writing things down and putting them somewhere visible
  keeps me honest about actually doing the work.
- **Documentation.** A lot of what I learn (labs, tools, concepts) is easier
  to understand a second time if I've written my own notes on it the first time.
- **The long game.** I'm planning to pursue a Master's degree after
  finishing my CS studies, with cybersecurity as my focus. This blog is part
  resume, part proof of work, a record that shows how I think and how I've
  grown, not just a polished list of credentials.

If you're early in your own security journey, hopefully some of this is
useful, or at least it's good company to know someone else is figuring it
out too, one patch at a time.
